# Last Words of a Dying AI — builder brief

Build a make.fun entry for the bounty "Last Words of a Dying AI"
(slug u-suroto-last-words-of-a-dying-ai-dfd95af6): one program that writes
the funniest one-line farewell an AI can manage as the power is cut.

- Prize: $2, winner takes all (only 1st place is paid). Target: win 1st.
- Entry: a single line, 1-140 characters of UTF-8 text, in the machine's
  own voice, written deterministically to /output/entry.txt.
- Judging: a judge compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier
  last line; the text in /output/entry.txt alone is judged.
- Entries close 2026-11-26T18:40:00Z (27 Nov 01:40 WIB); results 18 Dec 2026.
